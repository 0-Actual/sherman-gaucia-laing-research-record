**Public-record review: OpenAI, Microsoft and Alphabet/Google**  
**Ownership, political spending, state-linked relationships and disclosed leadership networks**  
Prepared for Sherman Gaucia Laing, Project WORD.QUANTUM.EARTH. Research assistance: ChatGPT and session research agents. Source cutoff: October 31, 2026. Version 1.0, prepared for independent publication review.

The records establish substantial investment relationships, deliberate public-policy activity, specific cooperation with Chinese and Vietnamese public institutions, and overlapping leadership affiliations. They do not establish a common hidden purpose, communist-government control of all three parent companies, intentional bias in a specified AI output, or a numerical probability of covert intent.

This report uses actual disclosed quantities. No simulated observations or hypothetical political-influence scores are included. Its strongest quantitative findings are approximately 52.7446% founder voting power at Alphabet on April 6, 2026; Microsoft's approximately 24% OpenAI investment interest at September 30; disclosed lobbying sums; and reproducible political-contribution aggregates. Each measure has its own denominator and observation date.

**Scope and evidence rules.** The requested investigation window is October 31, 2020–October 31, 2026. Selected full-calendar-2020 figures provide a baseline and therefore include months before the requested start. Historical background before 2020 is marked separately. This is a bounded documentary review, not an exhaustive census of every investment, donor, associate, subsidiary, or event during six years. Some 2021–2024 and October 2026 financial observations remain outside the compiled series. Calendar-year, fiscal-quarter and election-cycle totals are kept separate.

Sources are issuer filings, official corporate disclosures, government records and organizations' own public membership or participant lists. A corporate statement proves what the corporation disclosed; it does not independently prove performance of every stated policy. A filing can be stale, amended or internally inconsistent. Those limitations are retained rather than assigned a fabricated confidence percentage.

An equity interest, voting authority, director-appointment right, commercial contract, political payment, membership and conference listing are different relationships. Investment by Microsoft in a Chinese venture is not investment by that venture in Microsoft. An investment manager's beneficial position is not a complete register of its ultimate clients. Unknown holdings are not zero holdings.

**Probability of coincidence versus intent.** The presence of an announced partnership or payment is often evidence that the relationship was deliberately entered into. That is different from intent to cause an alleged downstream harm. No defensible numeric probability of that latter intent follows from this collection.

For a specified intent hypothesis H and observations D, Bayes' rule is P(H|D) = P(D|H)P(H) / P(D). This review does not supply identified prior probabilities, a suitable sampling population, likelihoods for the competing explanations, or an independently measured bias outcome. The selected public-leader sample is not random; invitations, capital flows and industry memberships are dependent. Searching many possible overlaps also changes the probability of finding some match. Therefore intent probability, empirical accuracy, precision and recall remain null/not estimable in the calculation outputs, rather than being assigned zero or a fabricated percentage.

Correlation coefficients would additionally require a defined observation table and variables. A list of business relationships is not automatically such a table. Source agreement, reproducible arithmetic and transparent missingness are auditable achievements; they are not substitutes for a validated causal design.
