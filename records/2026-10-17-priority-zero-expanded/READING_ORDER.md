## The broader mathematical question

The recurring research problem is how a formal or computational description survives translation into code, inputs, execution, measurements and reported claims. Review the definition, assumptions, domain, invariant, counterexample, observed result and correction together. Do not infer a universal root cause merely because two defects have similar symptoms.

Structured-state simulations can represent selected high-qubit families compactly because of restrictions on their state structure. That does not demonstrate arbitrary dense simulation at the same size, physical quantum hardware, or quantum advantage. Saved timing comparisons depend on workload, implementation, environment and measurement conditions. An optimization must preserve the specified result and be compared against a justified baseline.

## Current versus historical status

Preparation dates, recovered source dates, original requests, produced artifacts, executions and public disclosures are different events. Historical “private,” “hold,” “unsigned,” “not installed” or “awaiting review” labels describe their original snapshots. The new release has its own manifest and publication receipt; it does not rewrite or retroactively authenticate those earlier states.
