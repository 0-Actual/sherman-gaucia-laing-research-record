# Scope and review of this preview

This preview offers a research chronology, selected aggregate outcomes, corrections and open questions. The owner approved this partial preview for publication on October 16, 2026. Publication does not imply independent validation.

## What a reader can assess

A reader can assess the stated scope, unfavorable comparisons and distinctions between dates and evidence categories. These are selected research directions and reported results, not a complete inventory of discoveries or completed experiments.

It does not supply project implementation code, record-level inputs, fitted outputs or the full supporting execution record. Consequently, the preview alone is insufficient to reproduce the original numerical implementations, validate the historical timing comparisons or repeat the complete research program. Aggregate agreement is not a substitute for those checks.

The record distinguishes plans, prevented starts, executed calculations, unsuccessful tests, reporting errors and corrections. A stopped plan is not an executed failure; a reporting error does not automatically invalidate a successful calculation. Earlier mistakes remain preserved, and uncertain outcomes are not converted into successes.

## A defined route to further review

Researchers may propose a specific claim, identifying the evidence needed, comparison criteria, environment and result to be reported. Fuller implementation reproduction requires a separately agreed review scope and appropriate supporting materials. Not every historical source has been recovered, and not every requested replication is presently possible.

Independent review should distinguish reproduction of a saved calculation from a new measurement, descriptive inspection from physical validation, and an engineering improvement from a new mathematical result. Critical findings and negative results are welcome under the applicable project terms. The supplied license defines reuse permissions; this summary adds no broader grant.

## Limits that remain visible

The early chronology is incomplete, and concepts must not be confused with contemporaneous implementations. Selected materials findings have a reproducible descriptive subset, while the complete original analysis remains unavailable in the reviewed selection. The numerical comparisons have workload-specific boundaries. Historical resource readings do not isolate a system-wide causal effect. The mathematical review remains AI-assisted and requires outside scrutiny.

File hashes, where supplied, compare bytes against an identified reference. They do not by themselves prove authorship, historical priority, scientific correctness or who has read the work. No certification or guaranteed research outcome is claimed.

This owner-approved selection remains a concise preview and does not replace the complete archive.
