## Selected findings and unsuccessful approaches

**Numerical efficiency.** One retained comparison reported a ratio of approximately 65.85 between the earlier and revised aggregate solver times. A separate complete-request comparison reported a ratio of approximately 4.69. The conventional direct method remained cheaper. These results concern different timing boundaries and must not be combined into a general acceleration claim.

Two later comparisons reduced calculation time by 17.1% and 13.3%, while preserving the fitted outputs within each comparison. Both missed the declared 20% reduction target. An earlier revision reduced memory use but increased time by about 52%. The adverse comparison is part of the result, not an omission to be repaired by selecting only favorable measurements.

**Portable computation.** The retained work describes host-computer computation with portable software and data storage. Historical resource readings showed differences across observation windows, but changing background conditions prevent a causal claim that USB storage improved the whole computer. The selected evidence does not establish physical quantum-computer execution, general quantum advantage or measured electrical-energy savings.

**Materials data.** A selected public-data snapshot contained 1,103 records. Inspection identified 355 missing values for one reported property and 42 atom-count differences. A later diagnostic supported a repeated-cell interpretation for 40 of those differences and left two unresolved. A newly prepared descriptive recount reproduced selected findings against a dated, reacquired public source. It did not reproduce the complete original analysis or establish physical accuracy.

**Biblical source study.** The Hebrew corpus review distinguished 306,785 selected records across 39 books from counts of distinct linguistic words or a single chosen reading. Software corrections addressed a documented interruption-recovery defect. Structural checks, arithmetic agreement and software tests do not certify every linguistic annotation or establish a theological interpretation.

**Mathematical teaching.** Two selected educational manuscripts received complete reviews within their declared scope and separate AI-assisted cross-reviews. Missing explanations and assumptions were supplied, and earlier versions were preserved. The manuscripts explain established mathematics. This review is neither outside peer review nor formal proof-assistant verification, and it does not establish newly discovered mathematics.
