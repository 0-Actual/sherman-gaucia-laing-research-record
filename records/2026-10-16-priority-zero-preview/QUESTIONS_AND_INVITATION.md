# Reader questions and invitation

**What is being offered to the community?**

A concise account of research questions, selected development periods, aggregate
outcomes, mistakes and remaining uncertainties. The aim is teaching and informed
scrutiny. The owner welcomes serious criticism, including negative findings.

**Can I reproduce the reported experiments from this preview?**

No. This selection lacks the implementation, complete inputs and execution records
needed for independent reproduction. It supports preliminary assessment and
questions. Any fuller evaluation needs a defined claim, available evidence,
comparison method and separately agreed access to appropriate materials. Some
historical sources remain unavailable.

**Does this establish quantum advantage, newly discovered mathematics or industry copying?**

No. Classical quantum simulations are distinguished from physical quantum-computer
runs. The mathematical teaching material concerns established mathematics.
Dates and similarities alone do not establish copying, causation or priority over
another party. A comparison would need independently checked, contemporaneous
sources and precise feature definitions.

**What part does Biblical study play in governance?**

The owner identifies Biblical study as a source of values and purpose for the
project, including truthfulness, stewardship and accountability. The practical
research commitments are accurate attribution, preservation of mistakes, careful
distinction between evidence and interpretation, and peaceful use within the
applicable license. Scriptural interpretation is not used as a substitute for
scientific validation. The project does not claim ownership of Scripture.

**What support or collaboration is sought?**

The owner welcomes proposals for independent assessment, research funding,
computational resources, educational collaboration and appropriately scoped
commercial discussions. A useful proposal identifies the research question,
resources offered, duration, deliverables, evaluation criteria and intended use.
No budget, valuation, endorsement or guaranteed outcome is implied here.

**Does funding or downloading grant commercial rights?**

No additional permission is granted by this invitation. The
[existing license](LICENSE.md) specifies the permissions and limits for the included
material. Commercial use within rights controlled by the owner requires the
written authorization described there. Any further collaboration must define its
own materials, rights and obligations. The assisting AI cannot negotiate or grant
permission on the owner's behalf.
