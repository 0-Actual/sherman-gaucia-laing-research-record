# Earlier-work comparison

Part of **Evidence and accountability — money, policy and project comparisons**. November 2, 2026.

This comparison examines the October 31 corporate packet, published November 1, and the November 1 money-and-policy edition against the same failure classes. It is a bounded source review, not a claim that the earlier work was globally error-free or that every external source has been reverified. Research assistance and publication assembly were provided by ChatGPT/Codex.

## What prevents an “earlier work had no errors” conclusion

The corporate edition's separate `verify_google_ld2.py` uses assertions at lines 57–58, 65, 68, 70, 73 and 106 for filing details and totals. It is a live-source retrieval helper, distinct from the inspected main offline verifier. The earlier edition therefore cannot be described as assertion-free. No live fetch or optimized execution of this helper was performed for this source comparison. [Google filing checker](https://github.com/0-Actual/sherman-gaucia-laing-research-record/blob/cd40ae27cebca3b46d3eaa2b478bd8b86e5e17d6/records/2026-10-31-corporate-investigation/corporate/verify_google_ld2.py).

Its correction log also records an earlier extraction mistake: requiring a separator in the PDF's quarter/year text omitted a **$1,000 Texas Republican Legislative Caucus row**. The documented correction gives **74 rows and $537,800 for 2020**. The next entry records correction of a truncated recipient label. These are disclosed prior preparation errors, not newly reproduced failures of the delivered corporate edition. [Correction log, lines 13–14](https://github.com/0-Actual/sherman-gaucia-laing-research-record/blob/cd40ae27cebca3b46d3eaa2b478bd8b86e5e17d6/records/2026-10-31-corporate-investigation/corporate/CORRECTIONS.md#L13-L14).

The corporate publication reports 149 internal rational, decimal, count and policy checks. That historical result is not a claim of 149 external reviews, complete source verification or freedom from bias. Its publication review preserves unavailable sources, missing original response captures and unassigned external-review positions. [Earlier publication review](https://github.com/0-Actual/sherman-gaucia-laing-research-record/blob/cd40ae27cebca3b46d3eaa2b478bd8b86e5e17d6/records/2026-10-31-corporate-investigation/PUBLICATION-REVIEW.md).

The useful lesson is specific: retain explicit checks and measured counts, add the missing relationship and rendering checks, and preserve corrections and unresolved evidence. A better implementation in one file does not validate every other file. This comparison began as source inspection. The coordinator subsequently reran the main corporate suite and its manifest verifier, reproduced the old money-edition baseline and checking defects in isolated copies, and completed 22 new-edition regression invocations. The [saved validation receipt](validation-results.json) distinguishes these executed checks from the source-only review of the Google live-fetch helper.
