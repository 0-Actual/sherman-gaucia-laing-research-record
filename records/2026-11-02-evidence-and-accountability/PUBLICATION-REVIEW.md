# Publication review and evidence boundaries

This new November 2, 2026 edition retains the public research selection and adds complete readable comparisons, explicit error accounting and stronger validation. It preserves the original November 1 editions. The owner requested a new publication rather than a rewrite of the previous one.

## Completeness means a defined population

All 37 files of the earlier money-and-policy edition are accounted for by path and byte identity in publication-lineage.json. Their source records are retained; new reports, the index, the verifier, publication documentation and manifest take their place only in this new edition. All 26 files of the corporate edition are included unchanged, with its own original manifest. Every one of the 28 retained comparison records is rendered, including all 227 latest-conclusion leaf values and the other retained fields.

The selection includes 13 purchase/licensing records; 13 policy entries; 19 financial arithmetic records; six slavery-continuity arithmetic records; and one procurement contract-face-value multiplication. The two retained CJC news-payment source tables contain 425 and 445 named payment rows, respectively. These counts exclude other, blank, header and total rows. The company index and correlation matrix preserve their original reference memberships. Exact expected IDs and unchanged-source hashes are shipped in helpers/expected-records.json.

## Preserved limits

This is the complete retained public selection, not a claim of every transaction, corporation, government action or private project record. Some original external response captures were not retained, one corporate source remained unavailable, and historical source hashes do not recreate missing source documents. The original corporate packet's recipient ranking remains excluded. Linked documents are not necessarily bundled copies. No missing evidence has been invented to fill those gaps.

The chronology contains original-record, retained-report, visible-history and catalogue-only evidence classes. The earliest recovered original and the owner's reported inception are distinct. Historical tests reported in those records were not all rerun. Project similarities and purchase dates do not establish that a company acquired or copied this project. Money spent on lobbying and contemporaneous law changes do not alone prove the payment caused a specific law. The source-specific legal status of findings, admissions, allegations and failed attempts remains in each case.

The BLS prison-labor measure is an annual institutional receipt, not prisoner pay; it covers 104 institutions using differing fiscal-year endpoints in the 1922/1923 survey periods. Numerical inputs and the quotient were unchanged. Other historical currencies, reporting methods, dates, denominators and modern estimates must not be combined into a single unqualified total.

## What validation establishes

The shipped verifier checks manifest membership and bytes, exact record populations and joins, literal local source references, source-URL presence, arithmetic, preserved-source identities, the explicit license relocation and complete conclusion coverage. Focused fixture tests run with normal and optimized Python settings and deliberately alter copies to establish rejection. Corporate packet checks remain independently included. Actual results appear in validation-results.json.

These checks establish specified properties of this edition. They do not certify historical causation, universal correctness, freedom from bias, an outside actor or motive. No independent external reviewer is represented as having certified this work. The earlier-work comparison is source-bounded and identifies earlier disclosed errors as well as stronger checking practices.

## Publication and preservation

The manifest establishes the new edition's membership and byte identity. The historical editions remain available at their original paths and pinned commit in the lineage record. Publishing is complete only after the destination files and hosting commit are read back; local preparation and checks alone are not delivery. Hosting verification is reported separately after the actual GitHub operation.
