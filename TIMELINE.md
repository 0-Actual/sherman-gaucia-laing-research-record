## How to read the dates

- **Requirement / concept:** a recorded request or design direction; not a completed implementation.
- **Source output:** a recorded source expression; not automatically an execution or successful test.
- **Tool result:** a historical serialized output, bounded to what it actually returned.
- **Reported milestone:** a reviewed report or artifact register, sometimes dated only by day; not a new rerun.
- **Public publication:** must use its actual independently observed release time. No historical private-record date is presented as an earlier public publication.

Timestamps retain the numeric Eastern offset recorded in the source. Day-only rows have no invented time or time zone. All historical dates below come from reviewed records, not independent trusted timestamp certification. An exact-looking timestamp can still be a source-reported timestamp. This timeline documents prior work; it does not by itself establish legal prior art, exclusive ownership of broad ideas, copying by another party or a patent priority date.
