## Time and evidence model

The records distinguish owner requirements, assistant source outputs, tool outputs, later reviews, reported test results and public disclosure. A timestamp in an export dates that recorded occurrence. It is not an independently certified timestamp and does not automatically authenticate the contents, prove first conception or establish that an artifact was publicly available then.

Eastern timestamps preserve their numeric offsets; daylight time and standard time differ. No day-only report date is expanded into a fabricated hour, minute or second. The first recovered interaction is October 17, 2023 in Eastern time and October 18 in UTC; those refer to one instant.

The first publication field remains unset while this edition is prepared. When the documentary repository becomes publicly accessible, record the actual publication event separately. Never backdate it to the private research dates. A future independent timestamp or archive receipt can corroborate the public edition from its own date; none has been generated or verified in this preparation.

The chronology documents a research history and materials for evaluating prior work. It does not determine legal prior art, patent priority, infringement, exclusive authorship of broad concepts or copying. Similarity or temporal order alone is not a finding that another party used these private records.

## Disclosure and validation

Development source bodies, private conversation excerpts, private message identifiers, local filesystem locations, personal/family/contact records, biometric information, credentials and private image assets are excluded. Named algorithms are historical labels; their names are not claims that all described scientific behavior was validated. Source metadata is not executable source.

Human research direction and assistant-generated expressions are identified separately. The edition attributes the research program and publication to Sherman G. Laing while disclosing AI assistance. It does not silently convert assistant outputs into proof of sole human invention.

This preparation checks documentary consistency, selected row coverage and exclusion of private fields. It does not execute development code, create new content fingerprints, sign a release, authenticate historical signatures or verify current device status. Historical tests remain labeled as reports and retain their version-specific limits.

Source reports remain private. A reader can assess the disclosed chronology and its qualifications but cannot independently reproduce every private evidence check from this repository alone. Further evidence disclosure requires a separately scoped, owner-authorized release.
